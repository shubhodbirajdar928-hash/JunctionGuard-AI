Feasibility & Viability

Evaluating Tech Feasibility, System Vulnerabilities, and Countermeasures
04 // 10
Technical Feasibility
  • YOLOv8: Vehicle and pedestrian bounding box detection.
  • OpenCV: Frame handling & video parsing.
  • Pandas & NumPy: Accelerated statistical computations.
  • Supabase/SQLite: Real-time relational table logging.
  • Streamlit & Folium: Highly responsive GIS interface.
Challenges & Risks
  • Lighting Variations: Rainy conditions and night feeds reduce model visibility.
  • Missing Remote Data: Off-grid intersections lack telemetry connections.
  • Edge Compute Lag: YOLO pipelines lag during server deployments.
Mitigation Strategies
  • Preprocessed Filters: Adaptive thresholding to handle lighting changes.
  • Hybrid Sync: Local database caches (SQLite) acting as hot-fallbacks.
  • Zero-Latency Cache: Pre-computing telemetry indicators to eliminate processing lag.
